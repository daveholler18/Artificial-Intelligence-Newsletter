Claude

Claude is a family of next generation large language models and a versatile artificial intelligence assistant. It is designed to be a collaborator for individuals and enterprises that is “helpful, honest, and harmless”. Claude was developed by Anthropic, which is an AI safety and research company based out of San Francisco, California. The company was founded by Dario Amodei, his sister Daniela Amodei, and other former OpenAI executives. The former executives left OpenAI over strategic differences concerning AI safety and the company’s rapid shift towards commercialization. The primary focus behind Claude was AI safety and alignment. The developers came up with a training technique for Claude called “Constitutional AI”. In this training technique, the model is guided by a specific set of ethical and safety principals during it’s development. This helps achieve the developers goal of building an AI that is less likely to produce harmful of misleading outputs.

Popular Uses:

  • Analying Large Documents
  • Nuanced Writing
  • Automating Tasks That Require High Level Reasoning
  • Long Form Content Generation
